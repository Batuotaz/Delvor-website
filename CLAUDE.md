# Delvor Website

Delvor is an AI-powered automotive parts commerce platform. The website is a product vision and concept presentation aimed at strategic partners, catalogue providers, suppliers, and investors.

## Audience

Primary: strategic B2B partners — catalogue data providers, parts suppliers, regional distributors, investors.
Secondary: garages, workshops, and fleet operators evaluating the platform concept.

## Core problem Delvor solves

Buying automotive parts is confusing. Customers don't know OEM codes, fitment details, or which of multiple compatible options is correct. This causes wrong purchases, returns, and wasted time for buyers and sellers alike. Delvor uses AI to eliminate that confusion.

## Project stage

Delvor is in active development. The website presents the product concept and vision — it is not yet an operational platform.

## Page structure (7 sections + contact)

1. Hero — "Automotive Parts. Simplified by AI."
2. The Problem — complexity of current parts buying
3. The Solution — AI-driven VIN/description matching
4. How It Works — 4-step process (Identify, Analyze, Compare, Order)
5. For Suppliers — connecting supplier catalogues through AI
6. Partner Network — regional coverage and pickup/workshop integration
7. Vision — building the future of automotive commerce
+ Contact

## Content rules

The website must NOT:
- Mention specific partner, supplier, or catalogue provider names
- Include fake live product search results or pricing
- Include login or authentication UI
- Claim the platform is already live or operational
- Use startup clichés ("revolutionize", "disrupt", "seamless ecosystem")

The website MAY:
- Show illustrative UI concept sketches in SVG to convey the product vision
- Describe the intended end-user experience to contextualise the value for partners
- Reference regional market focus (Baltic, Lithuania, etc.) in the Partner Network section

## Tone

Professional, serious, modern, credible. Premium technology company presentation.
Large bold typography. Dark automotive + AI aesthetic. Gold accent (#C9A84C).
